資料與讀圖 / About this map

一天聲景依臺灣採錄時刻，顯示前60分鐘內錄音的聲音類型；錄音來自不同日期，並非同一天或即時聲景。
Time-of-day mode combines recordings from different dates in a trailing 60-minute window, using Taiwan local time. This is not a single-day or live soundscape.

300段錄音，每段前30秒由YAMNet分析。299段有可用時刻；其中34段依原站修正時區標籤、時分不變，1段仍待確認。
YAMNet analyses the first 30 seconds of 300 recordings. 299 have usable clock times, including 34 source-verified timezone-label corrections without shifting the clock; one remains unresolved.

片段比較以各錄音開頭對齊。顏色皆為模型推測，並非人工確認。漣漪只是視覺意象，不代表音量、信心或傳播距離。灰點不代表安靜。
Clip mode aligns recording starts. Colours indicate model predictions, not human verification. Ripples do not encode loudness, confidence or propagation distance. Grey points do not imply silence.

虛線表示所選網格、目前時窗內少於3段錄音。計數反映採樣，並非聲音發生率。點選位置，可聽原音與時間軸；人的感受由聆聽者自行填寫。
Dashed outlines indicate fewer than three recordings per selected grid cell and time window. Counts reflect sampling, not prevalence. Select a point to listen and explore its timeline; listener impressions are self-reported.
